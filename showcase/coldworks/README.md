# COLDWORKS: an AgenC Core demo

COLDWORKS is a one-minute first-person shooter mission that runs in Chrome. AgenC Core 0.19.34 wrote all of it, running DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at effort max, from the three prompts in this folder. Nobody edited its code.

## What is here

- **[PROMPT1.md](PROMPT1.md)**: the task.
- **[PROMPT2.md](PROMPT2.md)** and **[PROMPT3.md](PROMPT3.md)**: two playtest messages, sent afterwards in the same session. They say what looked wrong. They contain no code.

## What it built

- An abandoned mill at the edge of a ruined city: haze, light shafts, rain, wet concrete and rust.
- A carbine with a 2.5x optic and a sidearm. You can walk, sprint, crouch, jump, fire, reload and aim down the sights.
- Eight enemy soldiers who take cover, lean out, shoot back and react where they are hit.
- A self-playing demo mode (`?demo=1&seed=3`), which the video was recorded from.
- Every texture, model and sound is generated by the game's own code. It downloads no art and no audio. The whole site is 788 KB.
- Three.js and Vite, plus its own Playwright test with 30 checks, including real mouse and keyboard input.

## How it ran

- AgenC Core 0.19.34, the released build, in a container. Model: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, effort max. Headless (`agenc -p`), one session, three prompts.
- A recorder outside the container held the API key and logged every model call. The tokens and costs above come from it.
