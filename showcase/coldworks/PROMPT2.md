I played COLDWORKS on a Mac with a real GPU, in demo mode and by hand. It runs with no console errors, the mission reads well and the mood is right. Fix these, in this order of importance, and keep everything that already works.

1. Controls for a real player. In a normal Chrome tab where pointer lock was not granted, left click, R and the right mouse button did nothing (ammo stayed at 30/150, no zoom) and W barely moved the player. Start pointer lock on the first click with a clear prompt, and add a fallback for when pointer lock is unavailable: drag to look, left click to fire, hold the right button to aim, R to reload, WASD to move, Shift to sprint, C to crouch, Space to jump. Prove it in `verify.mjs` with Playwright's real mouse and keyboard: hold W for two seconds and check the distance moved, hold the right button and check the zoom, click and check the ammo count, press R and check the reload. Do not call game functions for these checks.
2. The first-person weapon. The rifle looks thin and floats at a steep diagonal in the lower right, and the hands are barely visible. Make it read like a real carbine held by gloved hands: larger in frame, a detailed receiver, handguard, stock, magazine and optic, natural idle sway, walk and sprint bob, recoil that kicks back and up and settles, and a smooth move to the center when aiming.
3. Enemies. They read as blocky dolls with pale masks. Give them realistic human proportions and gear (helmet, plate carrier, pouches, boots), muted colors, and animations: run, crouch into cover, lean out, aim, fire with a visible muzzle flash, flinch when hit, and fall convincingly when killed.
4. Water on the floor. The puddles are flat pale disks that look like spilled paint. Make wet concrete dark and glossy with irregular edges, reflections of the lights, and ripples where the rain falls.
5. The ruined city outside. The skyline is plain gray boxes. Make it look ruined: broken facades, window holes, collapsed floors, exposed rebar, smoke columns and haze with depth.
6. Rust. The red blotches on machines and columns look like red paint stains. Make rust subtle, textured, darker and more varied.
7. Realism overall: ambient occlusion and contact shadows, a muzzle flash that lights the room for a moment, bullet impacts and holes that stay, smoke that lingers, dust in the light shafts, and more detail on walls and floors.
8. Demo mode. The player never misses (100% accuracy) and snaps from target to target, which looks robotic. Make it look like a skilled human: small aim corrections, a few misses, recoil control, a pause to check corners, one reload under pressure and moving between cover. Keep it around a minute and keep `?seed=N` repeatable.
